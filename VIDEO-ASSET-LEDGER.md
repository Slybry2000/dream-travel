## Production replacement brief

- Deliver a matched set from one shoot, filmmaker, or tightly controlled grade whenever possible.
- Prefer 4K acquisition but export local web masters at 1920×1080 and a separate portrait/mobile composition.
- Each loop should be 6–12 seconds, silent, without cuts that call attention to the loop.
- Prioritize human interaction over destination establishing shots: shared looking, making, tasting, walking, talking, and laughing.
- Confirm model, property, artwork, and trademark releases for commercial travel marketing.
- Keep the existing local filenames when replacing clips so no layout or interaction rewrite is required.
