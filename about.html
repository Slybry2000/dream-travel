30+ Years of Expertise

At Dream-Travel International Corp, we believe exceptional group travel begins with exceptional people. For more than three decades, founder René Piard and our team have built a network of dedicated professionals who share one mission: creating meaningful, culturally rich travel experiences.

We are proud to work with trusted partners, coordinators, guides and local experts in every destination we offer. Whether it is Europe, the Americas or beyond, there is always someone on the ground who works directly with us to welcome and support our travelers.
