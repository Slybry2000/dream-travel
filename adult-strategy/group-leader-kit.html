How you are paid

  • Travel free.Once your group fills the trip, your seat is covered.
  • Earn a share.A set amount per guest who books, paid after the trip.
  • Give it back.Send your share to a charity or your studio fund.

Pick one later. Your numbers are agreed with you in writing before anyone is invited.

Can this cost me money?

No. There is no fee to host and nothing to buy. If the trip does not fill, it does not run, every guest is refunded, and you owe nothing.

Do I handle anyone's money?

No. Every guest pays Dream Travel directly on our own payment page. You never hold a deposit or chase a payment.

Will this feel like a sales pitch?

It should not. You see the price first, you can rewrite the trip description in your own voice, and anyone with a question talks to us, not you.

I have never organized travel.

That is the normal case, and it is why this exists. You know your people. We know the travel. You never plan a route or read a hotel contract.
