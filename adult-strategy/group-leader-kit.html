What leaders ask first

Can this cost me money?

No. There is no fee to host and nothing to buy. If the trip does not fill, it does not run, every guest is refunded, and you owe nothing.

Do I have to handle anyone's money?

No. Every guest pays Dream Travel directly on our own payment page. You never hold a deposit or chase a payment.

Will this feel like a sales pitch to my people?

It should not. You see the price before anyone else, you can rewrite the trip description in your own voice, and anyone with a question talks to us, not you.

I have never organized international travel.

That is the normal case, and it is why this exists. You know your people. We know the travel. You never plan a route or read a hotel contract.

Tell us about your group.

No plan required. A rough group size and a season is enough. We will tell you what is possible.
