What organizers worry about

The four worries, answered straight

Will this become my second job?

It should not. Your travelers book their own flights. Hotels, in-country transport, local guides, registrations, payments and supplier coordination are carried for you, and a point of contact stays reachable from before the first flight through the last dinner. What we ask of you: invite your people and be the voice they trust. Your job is your people.

What will it cost me?

There is no fee to host. Rene sets every trip’s price himself, after he has heard about your group, so nothing on this page is a quote. Hosting a group does not mean you take on anyone else’s cost.

What if people drop out, or I cannot fill it?

A fair worry, and no page can answer it for your group. Before you invite anyone, ask Rene how deposits, payment deadlines and cancellations work, and what happens if the group comes up short. Not sure of your numbers yet? That is fine to say.

Will it feel like a generic tour?

Every trip is built around your group: your theme, your pace, what your people have loved before and what they never want to repeat. The trips below are examples, not a catalog.

For you, the host

Hosting can give something back

  • Give it back. Redirect your share to a school, club or fund your people already care about.
  • Travel free. When your group fills the trip, your host seat travels free, earned once the booked guests you bring cover it.
  • Earn a share. A set commission on each booked guest you bring, if you want it.

Full terms are set up front in your host agreement.
