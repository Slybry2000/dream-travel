What hosting can look like

Pick the one that fits your group

  • A covered seat. Your host seat can be covered by the bookings of the guests you bring.
  • A host share. Hosts may receive a set share on each guest who books. The terms are in your host agreement.
  • Give it back. Your share can go to a school, a club or a fund your people already care about.

You see the exact terms in writing before you invite a single person. Host seats and shares are paid through the traveler price. What a host receives depends on how many guests book, and nothing is guaranteed. Hosting means bringing your own group. It is not a sales role.

How it works

You bring the people. We carry the rest.

  1. Talk. Rene listens first: who is coming, what your people loved on past retreats, what they never want to repeat.
  2. Shape. He drafts a custom trip around your pace and theme. We coordinate hotels, transport, local guides and suppliers.
  3. Host. A local partner we know receives your group and runs the days. Our team and the local partner stay reachable from before the first flight to the last dinner.
Straight answers

What hosts ask first

"I do not have time." You bring the group. We handle the planning and the supplier calls.

"What if not enough people sign up?" Eight travelers is enough to run a trip, and a first trip can stay small. Tell Rene the number you honestly expect and the trip is shaped around it.

"What does it cost me?" No fee to host. Host seats and shares are paid through the traveler price, and your host agreement shows the numbers first. Travelers book their own flights.
