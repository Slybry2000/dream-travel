Built around your people

Every Dream Travel trip is shaped to a host and their people. We start with a real conversation: who travels with you, how they like to spend a long afternoon, what your group has loved before, and what it never wants to repeat.

A custom itinerary is drafted around your pace and theme. Hotels, in-country transport, local guides, registrations, payments and supplier coordination are carried for you. Your travelers arrange their own flights; the trip begins the moment they arrive.

On the ground, a local partner we know and trust receives your group and runs the days. You focus on your people. We carry the trip.

Hosting has its own return

A trip is built around a host and the people they bring.

01

Travel free

When your group fills the trip, your host seat travels free, earned once the booked guests you bring cover it.

02

Earn a share

Earn a set commission on each booked guest you bring. What you earn scales with your group.

03

Give it back

Redirect your share to a school, a club, or a community fund your people already care about.

Full terms, commission rate and payout are set up front in your host agreement.
