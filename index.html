02 — Method

How a hosted journey gets built.

Every Dream Travel trip is shaped to a host and their people. Four quiet stages, no template, no rush.

  1. 01

    Listen first.

    We start with a real conversation. Who travels with you, how they like to spend a long afternoon, what your group has loved before, what it never wants to repeat.

  2. 02

    Shape the trip.

    A custom itinerary is drafted around your pace and theme. Wine and table; art and quiet streets; coastline and slow mornings. We choose suppliers we have worked with in person.

  3. 03

    Carry the logistics.

    Hotels, in-country transport, local guides, registrations and payments, supplier coordination — the details that turn a good idea into an easy trip to join. Each traveler arranges their own flights; the trip begins the moment you arrive. Your group sees one clean document, and we carry the rest.

  4. 04

    Looked after on the ground.

    In each destination, a local partner we know and trust receives your group and runs the days. A point of contact stays reachable from before your first flight through the last dinner — so you can host your people, not the logistics.

04 — Hosting

Hosting has its own return.

A trip is built around a host and the people they bring. In return, you travel, you earn, or you give it back — whichever shape fits your community.

  • Option 01

    Travel free.

    Your host seat, covered

    When your group fills the trip, your host seat travels free — earned once the booked guests you bring cover it. The structure is the same for every host.

    Full terms in your host agreement.
  • Option 02

    Earn a share.

    A set host commission

    Earn a set commission on each booked guest you bring. What you earn scales with your group — whether you host once a year or a few times a season.

    Rate & payout set up front.
  • Option 03

    Give it back.

    Send your share forward

    Redirect your share to a school, a club, or a community fund — a cause your people already care about. One quiet way the trip can help fund it.

    Your cause, your choice.
