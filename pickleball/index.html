Play where you've always wanted to go

Pickleball is the fastest-growing sport in America, and Europe is only beginning to discover it. These trips pair daily play and lessons for every level with the history, food and islands that made you want to go in the first place.

Groups stay small, usually eighteen to twenty-four guests. Hotels, courts, guides, ferries and transfers are arranged for you, and non-players are always welcome to come along.

On the ground, hosts who live and breathe the destination receive your group and run the days. You bring the paddle. We carry the trip.

Courts and culture,
in balance

Three hours of pickleball most days, with lessons, round robins and King of the Courts for every level. The rest of the day belongs to the place: ancient sites, island harbors, long dinners.

Players range from first-timers to tournament regulars. Not everyone in your party has to play.
