Reconnect with Nature in the Heart of Costa Rica

Nestled in the lush rainforest at the base of the majestic Arenal Volcano, this Yoga & Wellness retreat invites you to slow down, reconnect with nature, and restore balance to body and mind.

Over the course of six days, participants will experience daily yoga sessions surrounded by tropical landscapes, peaceful nature walks through the rainforest, and rejuvenating moments in natural volcanic hot springs.

The program has been thoughtfully designed to combine mindful movement, relaxation and gentle exploration of the region’s most beautiful natural settings.

From walking above the forest canopy at Mistico Arenal Hanging Bridges Park to discovering the beauty of La Fortuna Waterfall and unwinding in the thermal waters of Ecotermales Fortuna, each day offers a perfect balance between wellness activities and immersion in Costa Rica’s extraordinary biodiversity.

This retreat is ideal for travelers seeking a meaningful escape where yoga, nature, and relaxation come together in one of the most inspiring destinations in Central America.

Day 1 - Arrival in Costa Rica | Journey to Arenal

Arrival to Costa Rica

Arrival to Costa Rica at Juan Santamaría International Airport, where you will be welcomed by your private guide.

From here, enjoy a scenic private transfer through the lush countryside as you travel toward the beautiful Arenal Volcano area, a journey of approximately 3 hours and 20 minutes.

Upon arrival at Arenal Lodge, check in and take time to relax and settle into the peaceful natural surroundings.

The lodge offers stunning views of the rainforest, the lake and the Arenal Volcano, creating

the perfect atmosphere to begin your wellness retreat.

The evening will be at leisure so you can rest after your journey and enjoy dinner at your own pace

Overnight at Arenal Lodge, Arenal Volcano Area

Day 2 - Yoga & Rainforest Canopy Experience

Start the day with breakfast at the lodge, followed by a morning yoga session designed to gently awaken the body and connect with the natural surroundings.

Later in the afternoon, enjoy a guided walk at Mistico Arenal Hanging Bridges Park. This beautiful rainforest reserve allows visitors to explore the forest from both ground-level trails and suspended bridges above the canopy. Accompanied by a naturalist guide, you will discover tropical plants, wildlife and the incredible biodiversity of the region while walking through this peaceful environment.

Breakfast included

Overnight at Arenal Lodge, Arenal Volcano Area

Mistico Hanging Bridges, Arenal, Costa Rica.

Arenal Hanging Bridges are a series of suspension bridges in the Volcan Arenal National Park. Costa Rica is the central American country north of Panama that is renowned for its tropical birdlife, exotic wildlife and adventure pursuits such as whitewater rafting, parasailing and ziplining

DAY 3 - Yoga, Waterfall & Hot Springs Relaxation

After breakfast at the lodge, begin the day with another morning yoga class, allowing time for mindful movement and relaxation.

In the afternoon, visit the stunning La Fortuna Waterfall, one of the most beautiful natural attractions in the area. A guided walk through the rainforest leads to the base of the impressive waterfall, where you can admire the scenery and enjoy the refreshing natural surroundings.

In the evening, unwind with a visit to Ecotermales Fortuna, a serene hot springs sanctuary nestled in the forest. Relax in the naturally heated volcanic mineral waters surrounded by nature, followed by a delicious dinner at the property.

Breakfast and dinner included

Overnight at Arenal Lodge, Arenal Volcano Area

Day 4 - Yoga & Arenal Volcano Nature Walk

Begin the morning with breakfast at the lodge followed by a yoga session, offering time to reconnect with your body and the peaceful natural surroundings.

Later, enjoy a guided visit to Proyecto Asis Wildlife Rescue Center, where you will learn about local wildlife conservation efforts and have the opportunity to observe rescued animals in a safe and respectful environment.

Continue with a guided walk at Arenal 1968 Volcano View & Trails, where trails pass through old lava flows from past volcanic eruptions. The walk offers spectacular views of the Arenal Volcano and the surrounding landscape, while your guide shares insights into the geological history and natural ecosystems of the region.

Breakfast included

Overnight at Arenal Lodge, Arenal Volcano Area

Day 5 - Wellness Experience & Farewell Dinner

Begin the morning with breakfast at the lodge followed by a rejuvenating yoga session, allowing time to reconnect with your body while surrounded by the tranquility of nature.

Later, enjoy a guided walk at Arenal Wellness Park, where natural trails invite you to immerse yourself in a peaceful environment designed for relaxation and mindful exploration.

Continue with a cacao ceremony, a meaningful cultural and sensory experience that fosters connection, reflection, and well-being.

In the evening, gather for a special farewell dinner at Rufino's Restaurant, the perfect way to celebrate the journey and share final moments together.

Breakfast and dinner included

Overnight at Arenal Lodge, Arenal Volcano Area

A cacao ceremony is a spiritual ritual for cleaning soul, connecting with the nature, healing, mental wellbeing, mindfulness and self-exploration. People who join the ceremony consume ceremonial cacao.

Day 6 - “Pura vida!” 🇨🇷

Enjoy breakfast at the lodge before departing the Arenal region.

A private guided transfer will take you back to Juan Santamaría International Airport, with a travel time of approximately 3 hours and 20 minutes, where you will connect with your international departure flight.

Breakfast included

“¡Pura vida!”

In Costa Rica, pura vida literally means “pure life,” but Costa Ricans use it for all kinds of things—including goodbye.

It can mean hello, goodbye, thank you, everything’s good, enjoy life, or simply “all is well.”

So when you’re leaving someone in Costa Rica, you can absolutely say:

“¡Pura vida!”
