10-Day Pickleball in the Algarve

Introduction

Pickleball and culture in Portugal

Pickleball in the mornings and the best of the Algarve in between: golden cliffs, historic towns, a boat ride through the Ria Formosa, a live Fado performance and plenty of free time. Ten days, nine nights, based on the Algarve coast near Albufeira or Vilamoura. Avoid summer dates.

Day 1 - Welcome to the Algarve

Arrival and transfer

Arrival at Faro International Airport, meet and greet, and private transfer to the hotel.

Welcome dinner

Check-in, then a welcome dinner at the hotel.

Day 2 - Free day to relax

At leisure

Breakfast at the hotel and a free day to recover and enjoy the Algarve at your own pace.

Day 3 - Pickleball and Faro

Pickleball

Morning pickleball session (upon availability), with a transfer to the club and back to the hotel.

Faro

Explore the historic capital of the Algarve: the Old Town with its cobblestone streets and medieval walls, the Arco da Vila, Faro Cathedral and the Carmo Church with its Chapel of Bones.

Day 4 - Pickleball and Lagos

Pickleball

Morning pickleball session (upon availability), with a transfer to the club and back to the hotel.

Lagos and Ponta da Piedade

Walk the historic centre of Lagos, then see the golden cliffs, rock formations and Atlantic viewpoints of Ponta da Piedade.

Day 5 - Pickleball and a free afternoon

Pickleball

Morning pickleball session (upon availability), with a transfer to the club and back to the hotel.

Afternoon at leisure

The afternoon is free. Optional: a morning cooking class.

Day 6 - Pickleball, Olhao and the Ria Formosa

Pickleball

Morning pickleball session (upon availability), with a transfer to the club and back to the hotel.

Olhao and Ria Formosa

Visit the fishing port and colorful market of Olhao, take a boat ride through the Ria Formosa nature reserve, and see the old town and churches.

Day 7 - Pickleball and a free afternoon

Pickleball

Morning pickleball session (upon availability), with a transfer to the club and back to the hotel.

Afternoon at leisure

The afternoon is free.

Day 8 - Loule and Tavira

Loule

Visit the lively covered market and historic centre of this traditional market town.

Tavira

Castle of Tavira (exterior), the Roman Bridge and the Renaissance Church of Misericordia (included), followed by a live Fado performance (included) in a local church.

Day 9 - Free day and farewell dinner

At leisure

A full day at leisure.

Farewell dinner

Get together at the hotel reception for a farewell dinner.

Day 10 - Departure

Transfer to Faro Airport

Breakfast, check-out and private transfer to Faro International Airport according to your flight schedule.
